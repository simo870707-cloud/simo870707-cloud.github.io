Privacy Policy

1HOUR · NETSIM Software · Last updated 20 August 2026

1HOUR ("the app", "we") is a single-player, one-hour-per-day puzzle game. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. 1HOUR does not require an account, sign-in, or internet connection to play. All game data — your daily session/unlock timer, solved cases, dossier progress, and settings — is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device app storage.

  • We do not collect your name, email, location, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or share any data, because we do not collect any.

Device permissions

1HOUR requests the Vibrate permission only, used for haptic feedback during puzzles (e.g. confirming a correct entry). This permission does not grant access to any personal data.

Data storage & deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you control it directly: clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all saved progress. We have no copy of it and cannot recover or access it.

Children's privacy

1HOUR is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since the app collects no personal data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
